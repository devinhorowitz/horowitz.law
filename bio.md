## The career arc, written out

Continuous trajectory from age 16 to bar admission. Each role built on
the previous in a way that's only legible in retrospect.

**2005-2007 · ForTech, Forsyth County Schools.** Two-year internship at
South Forsyth High School during the senior years. General classroom, lab,
and faculty technology support. Installing and troubleshooting projectors,
printers, Promethean ActivBoards. Where the technical instinct started.

**May 2006 · CompTIA A+, age 16-17.** First real industry certification.
Earned during the ForTech years.

**2008-2012 · Classroom Support Assistant, Southern Polytechnic State
University.** Assisting faculty, staff, and students with webmail, learning
management systems, and distance learning software. Troubleshooting
mission-critical AV in classrooms — integrated sound systems, projectors,
displays, microphones. Concurrent with undergraduate studies in the same
building.

**2009-2010 · AITP at SPSU, Chapter Vice President then Chapter President.**
Student organization leadership during undergrad. Two-year arc into
chapter leadership.

**2007-2010 · B.S., Information Technology (Management Track),
Southern Polytechnic State University.** GPA 3.38. Minor in Business
Administration. AITP Vice President.

**2007-2010 · Pyrotechnician, Sky High Pyrotechnics International.**
Three years of contract work across Georgia, Alabama, and South Carolina.
Equipment transport into launch areas. Wiring runs, terminations,
continuity checks on electronic firing systems for legal and safety
compliance. Genuinely unusual for an attorney. Stays interesting.

**2011-2012 · M.S., Information Technology, Southern Polytechnic State
University.** Concentrations in Information Security & Assurance and
Healthcare IT. GPA 3.58.

**Mar 2012 - Jul 2014 · Educational Technology Support Specialist, SPSU.**
Promotion from student assistant to professional staff. Two years of
enterprise IT work that explains where the structural thinking comes from:
- Responsible for the wellbeing of campus-wide instructional technology
- Mentoring student assistants on projects and emergencies
- Developing technical standards for campus instructional technology
- Faculty and staff training on campus systems
- ITIL Release Management creation
- ITIL Change Management creation
- ITIL Incident Management creation
- SPSU/KSU Governance Bylaws Consolidation (during the school merger)
- Staff Governance OWG member

**Jul 2013 - Jul 2015 · SPSU Staff Council, elected Vice-Chair.** Campus
governance body composed of representatives from all areas. Mission
included giving staff a voice in shared governance and contributing to
institutional priorities and goals within the University System of Georgia.
Two-year elected role.

**2013 · Flip Classroom Design & Implementation, SPSU I Design Building.**
Project management on instructional technology redesign:
- Tables capable of accepting any student device for display on
  locally-mounted monitors for collaboration
- Display routing so any single table's content could be promoted to
  the entire class, or to individual tables in reverse
- All servicing equipment removed from instructional space; remote
  installation and maintenance

**Apr 2014 · Crestron DigitalMedia Certified Designer & Engineer (4K).**
Industry certifications in AV system design — the practical knowledge
behind the SPSU role.

**2014-2017 · Atlanta's John Marshall Law School.** Juris Doctorate,
*Cum Laude*, Top 10%, Peer Mentor. Law Journal Staff Editor &
Executive Technical Consultant (running the journal's tech infrastructure
alongside editing duties). Coursework deliberately bridging IT and law:
Electronic Discovery, Healthcare Law, Internet Law, Pre-Trial Practice
& Procedure, International Business Transactions & Writing, Wills, Trusts
& Estates.

**May 2017 · Distinguished Pro Bono Award.** 365 pro bono hours in
Spring 2017 alone.

**Nov 2017 · Admitted to the State Bar of Georgia (No. 551617).**

## What stayed in this file rather than the resume

The resume captures the credentials someone hiring an attorney needs to
see. This file captures the continuous trajectory underneath them — that
the technical instincts started in high school, that the pyrotechnician
work was real and concurrent with undergraduate IT studies, that the JD
was built on top of a decade of practical IT experience rather than
replacing it.

The hand-coded website itself is downstream of all this.
