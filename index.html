about

Civil litigation attorney with a decade of Georgia trial experience, focused on personal injury and complex liability matters. I handle cases from pre-suit investigation through trial — catastrophic commercial-vehicle and wrongful-death claims, negligent entrustment, motor-vehicle liability, premises, and coverage disputes.

My career has run through both sides of the docket. I spent most of the past decade on the plaintiff side — first at Ashenden & Associates, then six years at Greene Legal Group, and most recently as a partner at Hall & Lampros — before moving to insurance defense at Quintairos, Prieto, Wood & Boyer, P.A. The through-line across hundreds of depositions, mediations, and trials has been the same: build the file as if you're trying it.

Before law, I earned a B.S. and M.S. in Information Technology with a concentration in information security and healthcare IT. That background still shapes how I work — discovery technology, document automation, and the systems side of running a litigation practice.
